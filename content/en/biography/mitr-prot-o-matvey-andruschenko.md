Mitred Protopriest Fr Matthew Andruschenko (dean) (1899 - 1986)

<!--more-->

Around year 1949 Fr. Matthew Andrushchenko arrived in Canada from Belgium, where he served in one of the parishes of the Russian Church Abroad. On November 2, 1949, Archbishop Joasaph appointed Fr. Matthew as the rector of the parish dedicated to the Holy Trinity in Toronto.
